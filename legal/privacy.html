NextRep Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

NextRep ("the app") is a sports training plan generator. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

No account, no sign-up

NextRep does not require or offer account creation, login, or any form of authentication. There is no username, password, or profile tied to you.

What's stored, and where

All of your data — generated plans, workout history, ratings, game stats, performance benchmarks, and app preferences — is stored locally on your device only, in a local database. None of it is uploaded to or stored on our servers. If you delete the app, that data is gone unless you've used the in-app "Export My Data" feature to save a backup file yourself.

What is sent to our server

When you generate a workout or schedule, the app sends your selections (sport, skills, position, level, equipment, duration, and optionally a player name you typed) to our backend server, which forwards them to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the plan content. This request is not linked to any account or persistent identifier — we do not store these requests or their contents after the response is returned to your device.

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or any data for tracking purposes. We do not sell or share data with third parties.

Third-party services

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — processes your generation request to produce the workout content. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube — "Watch demo" and "film breakdown" links open a YouTube search in your browser or the YouTube app. We do not embed or track YouTube activity.

Notifications

If you enable the optional daily reminder, it is scheduled entirely on your device using the operating system's local notification system. No data leaves your device to support this feature.

Children's privacy

NextRep does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, since no account or personal profile exists within the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the developer via the contact listed on the app's App Store listing.
